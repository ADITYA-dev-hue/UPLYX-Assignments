About Pocket Ledger

A simple, private way to stay on top of your money.

Built for everyday budgeting

Pocket Ledger helps you record income and expenses, see your balance, and understand monthly spending without needing an account or a server.

Your data stays in this browser

Transactions and budget limits are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. Clearing browser storage will remove saved information, so export a CSV if you want a separate copy.

What you can do

  • Track income and expenses by date and category.
  • Search, filter, edit, and delete transactions.
  • Set a monthly budget and review expense summaries.
  • Export your transactions to a CSV file.
